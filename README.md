# ⚡ SURGE PROTECTOR ⚡

![MindRift Dragon](SurgeProtectorDragon.png)

A versatile power and magic limitation system for Aidungeon.com scenarios

### Your Powers. Your Limits. Your Story.

Tired of AI Dungeon turning your character into an unstoppable god? Want to actually have to **TRY** to achieve victory instead of simply narrating it? Want to be challenged?

You start as a novice wizard who knows three spells. Ten turns later, you're resurrecting the dead, teleporting across continents, and casually rewriting reality.

**Not anymore.**

**Surge Protector** is a character ability management system for AI Dungeon, designed to keep supernatural powers, spells, and extraordinary abilities believable, consistent, and under control.

## 🛡️ HOW IT WORKS

⚡ **Define Your Powers:** Choose from 25 built-in power types, register supported D&D spells, or create custom abilities with your own descriptions and restrictions.

⚡ **Set Your Limits:** Assign power levels from 1–10, each with defined capabilities, examples, and limitations. A novice and a master shouldn't have the same abilities!

⚡ **Natural Language Detection:** Describe your actions normally! Surge Protector recognizes common supernatural actions and provides the AI storyteller with relevant power rules and limitations.

⚡ **Two Gameplay Modes:** Use explicit `#power` activation for stronger scripted control, or play naturally with Mode 2's AI-guided recognition.

⚡ **Surge Suppression:** Attempt something beyond your abilities? Surge Protector guides the narrator toward appropriate limitations, resistance, or failure—without unnecessarily interrupting the story.

⚡ **Common D&D Spells:** Includes 20 supported D&D spell profiles with effect descriptions, limitations, and scaling, plus a larger spell reference index.

⚡ **Freedom to Experiment:** Creative uses of your powers are encouraged. Inventing entirely new abilities on the fly isn't!

## 🔥 POWER WITHOUT LIMITS IS BORING.

Surge Protector isn't about taking away your freedom. It's about making your choices matter.

A novice pyromancer shouldn't summon a supernova. A telekinetic who can lift a chair shouldn't casually throw a battleship.

**But they can certainly try.**

And sometimes, spectacular failure makes for an even better story.

## ⚙️ GETTING STARTED

Install the four JavaScript scripts in your AI Dungeon adventure and enter:

- `#POWER HELP` — Get started and view commands.
- `#POWER CATALOG` — Browse available power types.
- `#POWER LEVELS Telekinesis` — Compare abilities at every level.
- `#POWER ADD Telekinesis 4` — Register a power.
- `#POWER MODE 2` — Enable natural-language gameplay.
- `#POWER LIST` — See your registered abilities.

Surge Protector also automatically creates two in-game Story Cards containing instructions and a full command reference.
